## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at the **French-Azerbaijani University (UFAZ)** and a **Junior ML Engineer at the Azerbaijan Artificial Intelligence Center (NAIC)**, specializing in **Natural Language Processing** and deep learning research. I'm passionate about understanding how machines represent and process language — and equally eager to explore adjacent areas like computer vision, reinforcement learning, and beyond.

My background spans the full spectrum: from mathematical foundations to model architecture, from research and publication to production-ready mobile and backend applications.
