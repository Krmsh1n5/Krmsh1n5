## 🧑‍💻 About Me
 
I'm a Computer Science student at the **University of Strasbourg** and a **Junior ML Engineer at the National Artificial Intelligence Center (NAIC)**, specializing in **Natural Language Processing** and **information retrieval**. I like understanding how machines represent and search language — and building the systems that put those ideas into production.

My work spans the full stack of applied ML: from embedding models and retrieval research to the infrastructure that serves them at scale, with the occasional detour into remote sensing and astrodynamics.

- 🔬 Focused on **NLP & retrieval** — transformers, embeddings, hybrid/dense search, reranking, and RAG
- 🏗️ Building **production semantic-search systems** at NAIC
- 📄 Publishing across **NLP, SAR remote sensing, and astrodynamics education**
- 🤝 President of the **UFAZ Data Science Club**
- 🌱 Exploring **CNNs, GNNs, and Reinforcement Learning**
